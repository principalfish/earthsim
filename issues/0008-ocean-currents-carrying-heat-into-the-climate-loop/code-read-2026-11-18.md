# Code read for ocean currents (2026-11-18, main at 05ec69d)

1. **Sea vs land:** one temperature per cell, the sea-level air temperature (temperature.ts:5); the sea has no temperature of its own. A cell is land when `elevation − fround(seaLevel) > 0` (temperature.ts:743). Sea capacity runs from 2.5e8 down to the sea-ice 1e7 as the clear share falls (temperature.ts:1297; config.ts:243, 282). Spreading over sea uses diffusivity 0.55 against land's 0.35; an edge takes the mean of its two cells (config.ts:200, 224; temperature.ts:826). Ice albedo blends with clearShare, read from air cooled by the lapse rate (temperature.ts:845, 1295).
2. **Winds input:** `TemperatureWinds.flows` holds 12 months of 4·cellCount m²/s, from edgeFlows, on the solve grid (temperature.ts:283). Advection runs after the exchange step, flows interpolated by month (temperature.ts:1325). `createHeatAdvector(grid, airHeatCapacity)` uses r = cF/(AR²) and a per-cell `capacity` (advection.ts:110). A second field needs little change: pass c_air·F_air + c_sea·F_sea with c = 1, still divergence-free. Caveat: under sea ice the capacity falls to 1e7.
3. **Elliptic solve:** `createExchangeSolver` is CG with multigrid and needs every coupling > 0 (exchange.ts:381, 395), so coasts can't be cut, only pinned by a large per-cell mass. `removeConvergence` is global and would push flow over land (flowing.ts:189). A cell scalar's differences give gradient flows only; an exact streamfunction needs ψ on corners, but corners exist only per face (faceCorners grid.ts:674, sideCorners :714), with no global vertex index. `laplacian` at vector.ts:1456. No basin or connected-component code.
4. **Winds:** WindResult is monthly east/north per cell in m/s plus pressure in hPa (model.ts:19–55). No stress anywhere. `curl` exists (vector.ts:1405).
