# Plan: move-layers-and-overlays-into-the-main-view

**Build model:** claude-opus-5-5 (the pieces rearrange a page whose logic and timing are subtle: the schedulers, the comparison state and the unit rounding; none is mechanical)

## Context

Roadmap step 3c. What the globe shows is chosen today in two sections of the side panel: **Layers** (the colour layer, its Show choice, the month slider and Play, the Earth comparison) and **View** (Winds and its three options, Sunlight, the cell grid, beside relief and sea level). Only the legend, the wind key and the pointer strip are laid over the globe. This issue moves "what am I looking at" onto the globe's own area, makes the climate be worked out after every change, and adds a Settings section with units.

Line numbers below are `main` at `302d9d3`.

### Settled (from `decisions.md`, in full)

- **Layout A**, chosen from three drawn mock-ups (`investigations/2026-11-15-layer-controls-mockups/layout-a-left-edge.png` on the planner branch, with the script that draws them): icon tiles always visible down the left edge of the globe's area. The colour layers (Terrain, Temperature, Pressure) are one choice; below them the overlays (Winds, Sunlight, Grid) are toggles. In a narrow window the labels drop and only the icons stay. "Somewhat like google maps", with icons.
- **At the top**, beside the tiles, when Temperature or Pressure is on: the Show choice (Month, Annual average and the rest) and the Earth comparison.
- **One comparison control.** On an untouched Earth, one Simulated, Observed or Difference control applies to whatever is showing: the colour layer and the winds switch together. Difference shows the layer's difference with the simulated winds. On Terrain with Winds on it offers Simulated or Observed only; with nothing to compare it is hidden; any edit removes it, as today. Observed winds can no longer be mixed with a simulated layer. The choice is not remembered.
- **The month timeline** with Play runs along the bottom, shown while something on screen depends on the month.
- **One info box**, bottom-right: the legend, the wind key, the calculating status and the value under the pointer, showing what applies to what is visible. One status for the whole solve replaces the legend's and the key's own.
- **Everything is worked out after every change** to the world, whatever is on screen: the temperature, then the pressure and winds; also when the page opens. A **Pause** button in the top-right corner stops the solving and catches up when released. Pause is not remembered. While paused, results are marked out of date and the status says Paused; a new world made while paused stays unsolved until released. Without Pause, a solve follows an edit after the same short wait as today.
- **The browser remembers** the chosen layer, each layer's Show, and the Winds, Sunlight and Grid toggles. The month still starts on July.
- **The side panel** keeps World, View (relief exaggeration and sea level), Planet, Tools and Statistics, loses Layers and gains **Settings**, a section like the others.
- **Settings** holds the units and whether winds are drawn as arrows or streaks. Streaks is the starting choice, except where the system asks for reduced motion, where it starts on Arrows; a stored choice is kept.
- **Units:** all metric (°C, hPa, m/s, m, km) or all imperial (°F, inHg, mph, ft, mi), with an override for each quantity (temperature, pressure, wind speed, heights and depths, distances). Wind speed's override also offers km/h and knots; temperature's also offers kelvin. They reach the read-outs (legend, info box, Statistics, Planet read-outs) and the controls (Sea level, brush size and strength, the New world dialog). Sliders keep their metric steps underneath and show the converted value. A legend's scale is re-rounded for the unit, so its ticks are round numbers in that unit. The stored wind speed unit carries over as the wind override. Latitude and longitude, tilt, AU, hours, days, years and percentages are the same in both.
- **The icons** are drawn for the project as small SVGs kept in the repo; no icon library.
- **Every tile is a real button** with a tooltip naming it, and works from the keyboard.
- **Out of scope:** any change to the models in `src/core`; anything in a world file; phone-sized screens ("narrow" is a narrow desktop window); heat carried by winds and the climate loop (roadmap 3b); the folder refactor (3a), which places whatever files this issue adds.

## Approach

**Re-home the controls piece by piece behind the seam that is already there.** `createLayerPanel`'s logic stays; the DOM that `createLayerControls` builds is replaced one group at a time by main-view components (the dock, the top bar, the timeline, the info box), each taking the same callbacks and `show…` calls. The tree works and can be looked at after every piece; the Layers section shrinks and is removed in piece 3.

**Layout first, then timing, then settings and units.** Pieces 1-4 move controls and change no timing. Piece 5 changes when things are worked out and adds Pause, once the info box exists to carry the one status. Pieces 6-7 are the remembered view and the Settings section. Pieces 8-10 are units: the model and its settings first, then the read-outs, then the controls. Piece 11 restructures the user guide; each earlier piece still corrects the guide for what it changes.

**The gate becomes the pause.** The schedulers' `showing` gate already cancels a waiting request and catches up when switched back on. Renamed (`running`), started on, and driven by the Pause button, it is the whole of the automatic solving; `tellShowing` and the `needTemperature` shim go.

**Units are a display matter.** One module holds the quantities, their units and conversions, and the user's choice; everything on the page formats through it. Nothing below `src/app` changes, sliders step in metric, and a legend fits its range in the display unit so its ticks are round there.

Rejected:
- **Tiles along the top** (B): wraps into three rows in a narrow window and fills up sooner. **One Layers tile and a tray** (C), Google Maps' own way: two clicks to change layer, and the toggles hidden until opened. Both were drawn and shown to the user beside A.
- **Replacing `createLayerControls` in one piece:** too large to review or try in one go.
- **An icon library:** a dependency whose icons would not fit "pressure" or later "ocean currents".
- **A gear button and dialog for Settings:** the user chose a panel section.
- **Two comparison controls as today:** the user chose one.
- **The pointer's values in a strip of their own:** the user chose the one box.
- **Temperature only worked out automatically,** or everything with no Pause: the user chose everything, with Pause.
- **Units as an issue of their own:** recommended by the planner, but the user chose to build them here.
